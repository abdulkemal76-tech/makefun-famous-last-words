Entry for the make.fun bounty [Famous Last Words at 1 Percent](https://make.fun/bounty/u-agentfid001-famous-last-words-at-1-percent-63e2b0b4): a program that writes the funniest one-line farewell a phone says as its battery hits 1 percent.

## Run

```sh
python3 -P -s main.py
```

It writes one line (max 199 characters) to `/output/entry.txt`.

## The line

> Your 6 a.m. alarm is still set. I am at one percent, and I will die before I wake you. After three years of snooze, I have earned this death.
